Common questions

  • Where are my notes stored? On this Mac only, in one local file. Notesmith has no account, no sync service and is sandboxed without the network entitlement, so it cannot open a connection at all. You can also point it at a folder to keep a plain Markdown copy of every note, readable with or without the app.
  • How do I import from Evernote or Apple Notes? Evernote: import an .enex export, which keeps notebooks, tags, dates and internal links intact. Apple Notes: export to Markdown from Apple Notes, then import that folder and point Notesmith at the attachments folder so pictures, PDFs and recordings land back in the right notes.
  • I locked a block and forgot the passphrase. Can you recover it? No. A locked block uses Argon2id and AES-256-GCM with no account, no key escrow and nothing stored that could open it without the passphrase. Losing the passphrase means losing that block for good — this is deliberate, not a bug.
  • How do I delete a note? Deleted notes wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days before they're gone for good, so an accidental delete is always recoverable in that window.
  • How do I remove everything? Delete Notesmith from your Applications folder. Since nothing is stored anywhere but this Mac, that's the whole thing.
  • What does the MCP connector do? It's optional, and lets a connected tool search, read and add notes on your behalf. It runs entirely on your Mac, reaches no network, and never shares a locked block.

Privacy

Notesmith collects no data, has no account, and cannot reach the network — the sandbox entitlement that would allow it is simply not present in the app. Nothing about your notes ever leaves this Mac.
